About me

Hello. I’m Tim and Studio Brazier is my freelance design studio. For the last 12 years I’ve been working with charities, public sector teams, and non-profits, helping them make things better for the people they support.

I use design to make complex things simple, unusable things useful and poor experiences better, whether it’s a service, a product, a brand or real life experiences.

I’m currently designing new websites for a social enterprise and designing an app supporting humanitarian work across the world.

I’m a simple person, I love working with new people and learning about new challenges and using my experience to genuinely make things better.

If you’d like to find out a bit more about my work or want to understand how we could work together on a challenge you’re facing, just give me a shout on tim@studiobrazier.co and let’s start with a chat.
